Photo Cleaner — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-03 · Package com.cleanphotos.clean_photos_app · 한국어

Photo Cleaner collects no personal data. Your photos and any information about them never leave your device. The only time the app talks to the outside world is to check your purchase with Google Play (§5). There is no server, no account, and no advertising or analytics. This document states those facts item by item.

1. What the app does

It scans the photos on your phone, shows you which ones are byte-identical duplicates or merely look alike, and moves the photos you select to your phone's system trash. That is all.

Both the scanning and the moving happen entirely on your phone.

The app contains no code that sends photos or information about them off the device. It does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera permissions.

3. How photos are removed

The app never deletes a photo outright. It moves it to Android's system trash, where it remains in your gallery for about 30 days and can be restored by you at any point in that window. After 30 days Android deletes it permanently, and this app cannot recover it.

You choose which photos to move, and Android asks you to confirm once more before anything moves.

4. What the app stores on your device

One thing only: how many photos you have cleaned up under the free allowance — a number used to count against the free limit of 50.

No names, contacts, account details, device identifiers or advertising identifiers are collected or stored. Uninstalling the app removes that number along with it.

5. Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It is not a subscription and you are not billed monthly.

The app never receives or stores your card number or any other payment details. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

Your purchase is not recorded on the device — it is verified with Google Play. That is why it restores automatically on a new device or after reinstalling with the same Google account, and why a refund also releases the full version.

6. Advertising and analytics

There are no ads. No advertising SDK is included and the advertising ID (AD_ID) is not requested.

No analytics or tracking tools are included either. The developer cannot see what you do in the app.

7. Sharing with third parties

There is nothing to share. Nothing is passed to any third party.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 14.

9. Changes to this policy

If a change is made that causes the app to send data anywhere, this document will be updated at the same time and you will be notified inside the app.
